How to Play

  • Use the direction pad or tap an adjacent map cell to move. Moving into an enemy attacks it.
  • Tap a visible enemy to inspect it. Tap Ability, then tap a target, or tap Ability again to use the nearest target.
  • Potions heal HP. Scrolls refill MP and damage nearby visible enemies.
  • Weapons and armor auto-equip when they are better than your current gear.
  • Find stairs on floors 1-5, recover the Moonstone Chalice on floor 6, then reach the portal.
  • The game autosaves after every turn. If you fall, you restart at the current floor entrance with some XP lost.
