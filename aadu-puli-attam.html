Goats and Tigers

A game of hunting, blocking, and survival. Place your goats wisely and trap all three tigers.

How to Play

Objective

Three tigers hunt and capture goats. Fifteen goats work together to surround and immobilize the tigers. You control the goats; the computer controls the tigers.

Game setup

  • The tigers start on their traditional positions.
  • Goats begin off the board.
  • On each goat turn, place one goat on an empty intersection.
  • Once all 15 goats are placed, goats can move between connected intersections.

Goats

  • Tap an empty highlighted intersection to place a goat.
  • During movement, tap a goat, then tap a highlighted neighbor.
  • Goats move along lines only and never jump or capture.

Tigers

  • The computer moves the tigers.
  • A tiger may move to a neighboring empty intersection.
  • It may capture by jumping over one goat to an empty connected point beyond it.
  • One capture is made per tiger turn.

Winning

Tigers win after capturing 5 goats. Goats win when every tiger has no legal move or capture. Aadu Puli Attam, meaning “Goat and Tiger Game,” is a traditional strategy game from South India.
